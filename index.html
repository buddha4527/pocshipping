FAQ

Common questions

How quickly will I get a quote?

Most quotes are sent within a few hours on working days. Quotes for special equipment (reefer, flat rack, hazardous cargo) or unusual destinations can take a little longer because we check space and rates with the shipping lines.

What's the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL (Full Container Load) means you book a whole 20ft or 40ft container for your cargo only. LCL (Less than Container Load) means your cargo shares a container with other shippers and you pay by volume (CBM). As a rough guide, LCL is usually cheaper for shipments under about 13–15 CBM; above that, a 20ft FCL is often better value.

What details do you need for a quote?

Origin and destination, FCL or LCL, container type and quantity (or number of packages and total CBM for LCL), the commodity, approximate weight, cargo-ready date and whether you need pickup or delivery. Our quote form asks for exactly this.

What is included in the quote?

Every quote is itemised so you can compare: ocean freight, origin charges in the UAE, documentation / bill of lading fees and any surcharges. Destination charges, duties and taxes are shown separately or excluded, depending on the service you choose. The validity date is stated on each quote.
