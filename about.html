About us

Your point of contact in global shipping

POC Shipping helps traders and businesses move containers from the UAE — at competitive rates, with one person looking after every shipment.

Since 2012

POC Shipping was founded in Dubai in 2012 with a simple idea: make international shipping clear, affordable and personal. "POC" stands for Point of Contact — one person who knows your shipment from the first quote to final delivery.

Today we focus on container shipping (FCL and LCL) from Jebel Ali to Africa, Europe and other destinations worldwide. By working with multiple shipping lines and trusted partners at origin and destination, we can compare options and offer competitive rates on every route.

Our team works from Dubai with support from India, so enquiries get answered quickly.
