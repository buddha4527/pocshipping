Shipping to Djibouti

Djibouti is a key gateway for Djibouti and transit cargo to Ethiopia. We ship 20ft, 40ft and 40ft High Cube containers, as well as shared LCL cargo, from Jebel Ali and other UAE ports.

Cargo we commonly ship on this route

  • Machinery and equipment
  • Construction materials
  • Food and consumer goods
  • Transit cargo for Ethiopia

Transit time is an indicative port-to-port estimate. We confirm the sailing schedule and transit time with your quote.

Documents usually needed

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of lading
  • Transit documents for Ethiopia-bound cargo
  • Consignee / clearing agent details

Requirements depend on your cargo and can change. We'll confirm what applies when we quote, and your importer or clearing agent should confirm destination requirements.
