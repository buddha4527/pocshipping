FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about shipping containers with us.

How quickly will I get a quote?

Most quotes are sent within a few hours on working days. Quotes for special equipment (reefer, flat rack, hazardous cargo) or unusual destinations can take a little longer because we check space and rates with the shipping lines.

What's the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL (Full Container Load) means you book a whole 20ft or 40ft container for your cargo only. LCL (Less than Container Load) means your cargo shares a container with other shippers and you pay by volume (CBM). As a rough guide, LCL is usually cheaper for shipments under about 13–15 CBM; above that, a 20ft FCL is often better value.

What details do you need for a quote?

Origin and destination, FCL or LCL, container type and quantity (or number of packages and total CBM for LCL), the commodity, approximate weight, cargo-ready date and whether you need pickup or delivery. Our quote form asks for exactly this.

What is included in the quote?

Every quote is itemised so you can compare: ocean freight, origin charges in the UAE, documentation / bill of lading fees and any surcharges. Destination charges, duties and taxes are shown separately or excluded, depending on the service you choose. The validity date is stated on each quote.

Which countries do you ship to?

Our main focus is container shipping from the UAE to East, West and Southern Africa and to Europe. We also quote for the Indian Subcontinent, the GCC and other destinations worldwide on request.

How long does shipping take?

Typical port-to-port transit from Jebel Ali is around 6–18 days to East Africa, 25–35 days to West Africa and 25–40 days to Northern Europe. Exact times depend on the shipping line and routing; we confirm the transit time with every quote.

Which documents are needed?

Usually a commercial invoice, packing list and the bill of lading we arrange. Many destinations also need country-specific certificates (for example KEBS PVoC for Kenya or SONCAP and Form M for Nigeria). We'll tell you what applies to your cargo and destination.

Can you ship hazardous or temperature-controlled cargo?

Yes, on request. For hazardous (DG) cargo we need the MSDS and UN number. For reefer containers please share the required temperature. Availability depends on the shipping line and route.

Can you arrange cargo insurance?

Yes, marine cargo insurance can be arranged through our partners. Just mention it in your enquiry.
