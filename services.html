Services

Container freight services

Everything you need to move containers from the UAE — booked, documented and tracked by one team.

FCL — Full Container Load

Book a full 20ft, 40ft or 40ft High Cube container for your cargo only.

  • Rates compared across multiple shipping lines
  • Weekly sailings from Jebel Ali to major ports
  • Port-to-port or door-to-door

LCL — Less than Container Load

Share container space and pay by volume (CBM). Ideal for smaller or trial shipments.

  • Pay only for the space you use
  • Consolidation in the UAE
  • Usually cheaper below ~13–15 CBM

Reefer & special equipment

Temperature-controlled reefers, open top and flat rack containers for cargo that doesn't fit the standard box.

  • Reefer: share required temperature
  • Open top / flat rack for oversized cargo
  • Hazardous (DG) cargo on request with MSDS

Documentation & compliance

We arrange the bill of lading and help you prepare the right paperwork for your destination.

  • Bill of lading and shipping instructions
  • Certificate of origin
  • Guidance on PVoC, SONCAP and other destination rules

Pickup & door delivery

Container trucking from your warehouse or supplier in the UAE, and delivery at destination through our partners.

  • Pickup across the UAE
  • Destination delivery on request
  • Loading supervision on request

Cargo insurance

Protect your shipment against loss or damage with marine cargo insurance arranged through partners.

  • Quoted with your freight
  • Based on cargo value
  • Recommended for high-value goods
